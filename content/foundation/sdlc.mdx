## What this lesson covers

This module maps the **software development life cycle** and the points where QA adds the most value.

- Common SDLC models
- Where testing sits in each phase
- How QA partners with product and engineering

## Outcome

After this lesson you will be able to explain where testing belongs in a delivery cycle.
